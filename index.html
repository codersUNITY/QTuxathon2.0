About

QTUXATHON 2.0 is a 24-hour, non-stop hackathon by the Department of CSE – Cyber Security. It’s all about pushing your limits, solving tough challenges, and working with a team to create something epic. Expect sleepless nights, intense coding, and a lot of fun along the way.
This is not your average event—it's raw, it's chaotic, and it’s here to test your skills under pressure. If you’re ready for the grind, we’ve got you covered with food, entertainment, and a chance to make an impact.
