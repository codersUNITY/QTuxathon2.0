Frequently Asked Questions

What even is a hackathon?

A hackathon is where you turn your crazy ideas into real projects. We provide hardware, mentors, fun activities, speakers, workshops, food, friends, and so much more. You take care of hacking; we’ll take care of you.

How do I reach the venue?

Just show up Here [ Ohh Hii ] – it’s the place we spammed all over the posters. Still lost? Just follow the swarm of nerds with laptops and dark circles under their eyes.

Is there a dress code?

Wear whatever keeps you comfy. Pajamas, hoodies, full-on cosplay – as long as you’re dressed, we’re chill.

What can I build?

Literally anything – from a to-do list app that roasts you to a fridge that tweets. Just don’t build another ChatGPT clone or Skynet. We like Earth, thanks.

What if I have never coded?

Perfect. We welcome chaos. Come learn, vibe, and wing it like everyone else. We'll have mentors, friends, and a LOT of Stack Overflow.

Do I get cool hardware?

Maybe. Maybe not. Depends on what you mean by "cool". Bring your own stuff if you're fancy. We'll see what we can offer.

Do you pay for my travel?

We're trying our best to sort that out. No promises – but we’ll update if free rides are on the table.

Who can attend?

If you're a student or a fresh-out-the-box graduate (within 1 year), and you're 18+, you’re in!

Is there a Code of Conduct?

Of course. TLDR: Don’t be a jerk. Respect others. No creepy stuff. MLH Code of Conduct applies – follow it or get booted.

what is MLH code of conduct?

Yes! We will be following the MLH Code of Conduct.
TLDR: Basically: "Don't be an a**hole. Respect the vibe."

Still confused?

Yeah Mail us your questions. Or better yet, just DM us on Insta like everyone else.

What should I bring with me?

Your laptop, charger, ID card, and anything else that keeps you functioning. Bonus points if you bring good energy and meme supplies.

What’s the food situation?

Oh, you’ll thrive. We’ve got meals, snacks, drinks, and enough caffeine to power a small city. You won't go hungry, we promise.
